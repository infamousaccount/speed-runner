Speed Stars Game Overview

Speed Stars is a fast-paced athletics racing game. Control your character's running rhythm, compete in sprint events, and try to achieve your fastest time.

How to Play Speed Stars

Alternate between the left and right controls to accelerate your runner. Timing and rhythm are important to maintaining your speed.

PC and Mobile Controls

PC: Use A and D or the arrow keys. Mobile: Tap the LEFT and RIGHT buttons.

Tips and Tricks

Maintain a steady rhythm, avoid tapping too quickly, and practice to improve your sprint times.
